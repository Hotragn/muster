Muster lives at the pharmacy counter.

Amber vials, off-white thermal labels and the teal ink of a pharmacist's notes. Data is set like a printed Rx label; everything people read is set in a typeface built for low-vision readers.

Logo

Signature element: the muster line

Scattered dots are bottles due on different days. Dots on the line share a pickup day. It is the logo, the timeline's pickup marker, the line-up animation and the edge of the pharmacist card.
