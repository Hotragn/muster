# NextSpark Hacks — event facts

Source: https://nextspark-hacks.devpost.com/ (Overview), /rules, /details/dates, /updates, /forum_topics, /project-gallery. All read 2026-10-26 ~05:20 EDT.

## Eligibility
- Ages 13+, **students only**. Companies/professional organizations excluded. All countries except Devpost standard exceptions.
- Individuals or teams of up to 4. Organizers and judges may not compete.

## Theme
Open ended, "Beginner Friendly", "Machine Learning/AI" tags. "Build a working prototype that addresses a real-world problem." Prompt question: **"Who could this help, and how?"** Website, mobile app, game, AI tool or other software.

## Submission period and deadline
- Submissions: Oct 21, 2026 11:00am CDT → **Oct 26, 2026 10:00pm CDT**
- Judging: Oct 27 9:00am – Oct 29 5:00pm CDT. Winners announced Oct 30 9:00pm CDT.
- Projects must be created during the submission period (planning beforehand allowed).

## Required submission items
1. Project description: problem, who it helps, what it does, how built, challenges, what was learned.
2. Public GitHub repo (or equivalent) with setup instructions.
3. Demo video 1–3 minutes, viewable without requesting access.
4. At least one screenshot.
5. Tools and credits: technologies, external resources, AI assistance used.
- Optional: live demo link. Must clearly identify incomplete features and simulated functionality.

## Judging criteria (exact wording, Overview page)
- **Creativity and Originality** — "Does the project bring a fresh idea or a thoughtful new approach to an existing problem?"
- **Impact** — "Does the project address a meaningful problem? Is it clear who would benefit and how?"
- **Technical Execution** — "Does the prototype work as demonstrated? How well are its features built and connected?"
- **Design and User Experience** — "Is the project easy to understand and use? Does its design make it accessible to its intended audience?"
- Rules page: "Projects will be judged on what is demonstrated and submitted, rather than promised future features."
- Judges listed: Pall Leuva, Adwaith Renjith.

## AI tools rule
Allowed for coding, design, research. Must explain use and distinguish own contributions from generated work; must be able to explain the project.

## Other rules
- Credit libraries, assets, datasets. No passwords/API keys/private info in the submission.
- Plagiarism, false claims, misrepresenting team contributions → possible disqualification. Organizers may request a demonstration.
